## ⚙️ Foundational Architecture: AI Model vs. AI Agent

* **Architecture (Component vs. System):** An **AI Model** is a core mathematical artifact (weights and parameters) trained to recognize patterns and predict tokens from a direct input. An **AI Agent** is an orchestration system that embeds an AI model as its reasoning engine alongside memory, goal-setting logic, and external tool integrations.
* **Execution Paradigm (Stateless Inference vs. Iterative Control Loop):** An AI Model executes in a single-turn, stateless request-response cycle; its operation terminates immediately after producing an output. An AI Agent operates via an autonomous feedback loop (perceive $\rightarrow$ plan $\rightarrow$ act $\rightarrow$ evaluate), dynamically decomposing high-level objectives into sequential multi-step tasks.
* **Environment Interaction (Generation vs. Action Execution):** An AI Model is passive and isolated from environments, unable to alter external states. An AI Agent interacts with digital environments by autonomously invoking APIs, querying databases, executing workflows, and adapting to runtime errors to achieve a specified goal.

## 🎯 Strategic Takeaway
A power user transitions from treating AI as a *conversational chatbot (Model)* to leveraging it as an *autonomous executor (Agentic System)* wrapped around deterministic tools and real-world inputs.
